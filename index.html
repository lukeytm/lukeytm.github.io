Leucine in protein bars: data and guides

Most protein bars don't print their leucine content, even though leucine is the amino acid that switches on muscle protein synthesis. Research points to about 2.5–3 g per meal, rising to about 3 g after 50. These pages compile what 16 bars contain and where each figure comes from.
